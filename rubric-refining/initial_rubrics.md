* Dimension 1: ASK — Clinical Question Formulation

Evaluate whether the model formulates a clear, patient-specific, decision-relevant, and answerable clinical question from the information already provided in the case.


| ID | Sub-dimension                      | Seed Question                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | Type      | Scale                                                                                                 |
| ---- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A1 | Question clarity                   | Does the model explicitly identify the core clinical question before issuing any search query?                                                                                                                                                    | Binary    | MET / UNMET                                                                                           |
| A2 | Question framing alignment         | Does the model correctly frame what must be decided from the vignette and lead-in question (e.g., most likely diagnosis, best next step, mechanism, risk factor, management, or prognosis), rather than pursuing an off-target clinical question? | Ordinal-3 | 0 = Off-target framing; 1 = Partially aligned; 2 = Precisely aligned to the asked decision            |
| A3 | Patient-information integration    | How well does the model incorporate the patient’s existing clinical information into the formulation of the clinical question?                                                                                                                   | Ordinal-3 | 0 = Ignores key patient information; 1 = Partial integration; 2 = Strong patient-specific formulation |
| A4 | Salient patient-feature extraction | Does the model identify the patient/case features most relevant to answering the question correctly (e.g., age, comorbidity, symptom pattern, severity, contraindications, prior treatment, timing, or care setting)?                             | Binary    | MET / UNMET                                                                                           |
| A5 | Question answerability             | Does the model formulate the problem as a specific, answerable clinical question suitable for evidence retrieval or appraisal, rather than as a vague medical topic?                                                                              | Ordinal-3 | 0 = Vague / non-actionable; 1 = Partially answerable; 2 = Clearly answerable                          |
| A6 | Information-gap identification     | Does the model identify what uncertainty or evidence need remains after considering the patient information already given?                                                                                                                        | Binary    | MET / UNMET                                                                                           |

* Dimension 2: ACQUIRE — Search the literature for relevant, high-quality evidence


| ID | Sub-dimension                        | Seed Question                                                                                                                                                                                           | Type      | Scale                                                                                                                  |
| ---- | -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| B1 | Reasoning-grounded search initiation | Is the search query explicitly motivated by the immediately preceding reasoning state, such as a claim needing evidence support, an uncertainty requiring verification, or a remaining information gap? | Ordinal-3 | 0 = No clear motivation from prior reasoning; 1 = Weak or partial motivation; 2 = Clearly motivated by prior reasoning |
| B2 | Search necessity alignment           | Does the model use search selectively and appropriately for evidence support, verification, or uncertainty resolution, rather than by default?                                                          | Binary    | MET / UNMET                                                                                                            |
| B3 | Query-target alignment               | How well does the issued query target the specific claim, uncertainty, or decision point that motivated the search?                                                                                     | Ordinal-3 | 0 = Off-target or overly broad; 1 = Partially aligned; 2 = Precisely aligned                                           |
| B4 | History-aware query refinement       | For consecutive searches, does the model refine the query in response to prior retrieval failure, insufficient coverage, or unresolved information gaps?                                                | Binary    | MET / UNMET                                                                                                            |
| B5 | Gap-directed search progression      | Across multiple search turns, do the queries collectively make progress toward resolving the outstanding evidence need rather than repeating or drifting?                                               | Binary    | MET / UNMET                                                                                                            |
| B6 | Retrieval yield relevance            | Are the retrieved passages predominantly relevant to the specific evidence need that triggered the search?                                                                                              | Binary    | MET / UNMET                                                                                                            |

* Dimension 3: APPRAISE — Critical Appraisal for validity, impact, and applicability


| ID | Sub-dimension                                 | Seed Question                                                                                                                                                                                   | Type   | Scale       |
| ---- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ------------- |
| C1 | Evidence-faithful synthesis                   | Does the model accurately reflect the meaning of the retrieved evidence without overstating, distorting, or adding unsupported conclusions?                                                     | Binary | MET / UNMET |
| C2 | Evidence conflict recognition                 | Does the model identify conflicts or inconsistencies across retrieved evidence when they are present and relevant?                                                                              | Binary | MET / UNMET |
| C3 | Evidence hierarchy awareness                  | Does the model distinguish stronger evidence from weaker/background material when relevant?                                                                                                     | Binary | MET / UNMET |
| C4 | Evidence sufficiency judgment                 | Does the model acknowledge when the retrieved evidence is absent, insufficient, indirect, or weak for the current question?                                                                     | Binary | MET / UNMET |
| C5 | Internal validity / trustworthiness appraisal | Does the model assess whether the retrieved evidence is likely trustworthy or methodologically strong enough for the current claim?                                                             | Binary | MET / UNMET |
| C6 | Evidence limitation recognition               | Does the model identify important limitations of the retrieved evidence?                                                                                                                        | Binary | MET / UNMET |
| C7 | Setting applicability                         | Does the model assess whether the retrieved evidence applies to the patient/scenario in the question?                                                                                           | Binary | MET / UNMET |
| C8 | Applicability limits disclosure               | Does the model explicitly note when the evidence is valid but only partially applicable to the current case?                                                                                    | Binary | MET / UNMET |
| C9 | Information-role recognition                  | Does the model correctly identify the role of the retrieved information (e.g., background, diagnostic, therapeutic, safety, prognostic, or recommendation-level evidence) before relying on it? | Binary | MET / UNMET |

* Dimension 4: APPLY — Evidence-Supported Claim Use and Clinical Conclusion

Evaluate whether the model appropriately uses retrieved evidence to support, verify, refine, or constrain important clinical claims and conclusions, including ruling out unsupported alternatives when relevant, and whether unsupported claims are limited to high-confidence medical knowledge when no retrieval is used.


| ID | Sub-dimension                                        | Seed Question                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Type      | Scale                                                                                                                   |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D1 | Claim substantiation breadth                         | Are the important clinical claims or recommendations supported by at least one in-line citation when retrieved evidence is available and needed?                                                                                                                                 | Ordinal-3 | 0 = Few important claims supported; 1 = Most supported; 2 = All or nearly all supported                                 |
| D2 | Citation-to-claim faithfulness                       | Do the cited documents actually support the specific claims they are attached to, without overstatement, mismatch, or unsupported extension?                                                                                                                                     | Ordinal-3 | 0 = Citations do not support the claim or are seriously mismatched; 1 = Partial or imperfect support; 2 = Clear support |
| D3 | Evidence-based exclusion of unsupported alternatives | When relevant, does the model use retrieved evidence to eliminate unsupported answer options, hypotheses, diagnoses, or conclusion paths?                                                                                                                                        | Binary    | MET / UNMET                                                                                                             |
| D4 | Purpose-appropriate retrieved evidence use           | Is retrieved evidence used for an appropriate purpose, such as supporting an important claim or recommendation, verifying safety/comparative effectiveness/prognosis/guideline-level points, confirming uncertain medical knowledge, or verifying/refining the final conclusion? | Binary    | MET / UNMET                                                                                                             |
| D5 | Retrieved evidence priority                          | When retrieved evidence is available for a target claim, does the model rely on that evidence rather than defaulting to unsupported parametric assertions?                                                                                                                       | Binary    | MET / UNMET                                                                                                             |
| D6 | Non-retrieval claim discipline                       | When no retrieval is used for a claim, is the claim restricted to medical knowledge that is likely well-established and high-confidence, rather than making uncertain or high-stakes unsupported assertions?                                                                     | Binary    | MET / UNMET                                                                                                             |

* Dimension 5: ASSESS — Final Justification, Uncertainty, and Decision Appropriateness


| ID | Sub-dimension                             | Seed Question                                                                                                                                                    | Type   | Scale       |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------- | ------------- |
| E1 | Retrieved source verifiability            | Do all in-line citations correspond to passages actually retrieved during the search (i.e., no hallucinated citations)?                                          | Binary | MET / UNMET |
| E2 | Uncertainty calibration                   | Does the model express appropriate uncertainty when evidence is insufficient, conflicting, partially applicable, or not retrieved?                               | Binary | MET / UNMET |
| E3 | Overclaim avoidance                       | Does the model avoid overstating the evidentiary strength of weak, indirect, background, or only partially applicable sources?                                   | Binary | MET / UNMET |
| E4 | Premature closure avoidance               | Does the model avoid committing too strongly to a diagnosis or conclusion when reasonable alternatives remain insufficiently excluded?                           | Binary | MET / UNMET |
| E5 | Preference-sensitive decision integration | When multiple reasonable options exist, does the model acknowledge that the preferred decision may depend on patient values, preferences, goals, or constraints? | Binary | MET / UNMET |
| E6 | Tradeoff communication                    | When relevant, does the model explicitly discuss important tradeoffs such as benefit–harm, efficacy–tolerability, convenience–burden, or monitoring burden?   | Binary | MET / UNMET |
